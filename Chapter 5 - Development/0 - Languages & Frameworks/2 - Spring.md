# Spring

## Overview

In this part, you'll learn Spring Framework fundamentals and Spring Boot.

## Goals

- Understand Inversion of Control and Dependency Injection, and how Spring implements them.
- Understand beans, the Spring container, and the ways to configure and inject them.
- Understand what Spring Boot adds on top of Spring.

## Outcome

Study questions:

1. What is Spring? Why do we need it?
2. What are IoC and DI?
3. How does Spring implement the DI and IOC patterns? How does its container work with beans? What actions does it perform?
4. What is Spring ApplicationContext and Spring BeanFactory?
5. In what ways can Spring do DI?
6. What is a Bean? And how do I define them?
7. What is Autowire? In what ways can I do Autowire for a specific bean? How does Spring know which bean to inject?
8. Can I create two beans of the same type? If so, how does Spring know which one to inject?
9. What is `@Component`, and how is it different from `@Bean`? What does `@ComponentScan` do?
10. What is @Configuration? And what is the purpose of classes with this annotation?
11. What is Spring Boot?
12. What do `@SpringBootApplication` and `@EnableAutoConfiguration` do?
13. What are Spring Boot Starters, and what problem do they solve?
