# Pyroscope

## Overview

Pyroscope is Grafana's open-source continuous profiling platform - it captures where an application spends CPU time and memory over time, rounding out metrics, logs, and traces as our fourth observability signal. This part covers what continuous profiling gives you, then points you at official resources and videos.

## Goals

- Understand what continuous profiling is and how it differs from a one-off profiling snapshot.
- Understand Pyroscope's high-level architecture (how it collects and stores profiling data).
- Get comfortable reading a flame graph in Grafana.

## Outcome

1. What is continuous profiling, and what problem does it solve that CPU/memory metrics alone don't?
2. What is a flame graph, and how do you read one?
3. At a high level, how does Pyroscope collect profiling data from an application, and how does that data end up visualized in Grafana?
