# Observability Platforms & APM

## Overview

Once telemetry is collected, platforms turn it into something teams can act on. This part covers APM, the reliability concepts built on top of telemetry (such as SLOs), monitoring from the user's side, and how to compare observability platforms.

## Goals

- Learn about different Observability platforms.
- Understand how we can choose the right solution based on our system.
- Understand what APM is and how it relates to observability.
- Learn some more Observability concepts: SLIs/SLOs/SLAs, error budgets, golden signals, RUM, and alerting.

## Outcome

The outcome of this part is a detailed markdown explaining the concepts you have learned in this part. Your markdown should also contain comparison between different Observability platforms.

In your markdown make sure there is an answer to the following questions:

1. What is APM (Application Performance Monitoring)? How does it relate to observability? What does a typical APM product show you?
2. Explain the difference between SLI, SLO, SLA, their relation and how observability helps with each of them
    - Explain the importance of each in depth.
    - What is an error budget, and how do teams use it?
3. What are KPIs? How do they relate to SLI, SLO and SLA? How do they differ?
4. What are golden signals? Explain each one of them. How do they compare to the RED metrics from the Signals part, and to the USE method?
5. Explain the differences between Synthetic monitoring & RUM, where they differ and where they are alike.
6. Explain the concept of Session Replay. What does it let you see that other kinds of monitoring don't?
7. Choose 4 features that RUM has, and explain their usage. How do they take place in the real-world?
8. What is MTTD and MTTR?
9. What are alerts? Why are they important? What is an alert manager?
10. List 2 more insights Observability gives your system and explain them.
11. Provide a list of at least 3 on-prem observability solutions and 3 cloud observability solutions that exist today with comparison between them.
